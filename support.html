Lafa Support

Lafa is a European Portuguese verb conjugation quiz app for beginner (A1-A2) learners.

Contact

For help, questions, or feedback, email aruhicen@gmail.com. Lafa is built and supported by a solo developer, so replies are best-effort.

Reporting a problem with a question

If a quiz question looks wrong, use the "Report a problem" control on the quiz screen — it sends the exact question straight to the developer and is the fastest way to get a content error fixed. The app also has a general feedback control for anything else you want to report.

Common questions

Is Lafa free? Yes — Lafa is free with no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account? No — there is no login or account of any kind.

Does Lafa teach Brazilian Portuguese? No — Lafa teaches European Portuguese (Portugal), not Brazilian Portuguese.

Does Lafa work without an internet connection? Yes — if the server is unreachable, Lafa falls back to bundled content on the device so you can keep practicing.
