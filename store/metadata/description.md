Lafa is a verb-conjugation quiz for beginner (A1-A2) learners of European
Portuguese. It drills conjugation until the correct form is automatic,
without lessons, vocabulary lists, or reading exercises.

## How it works

Pick one or more tenses and a verb mode, then start a 10-question
multiple-choice quiz. Each question shows a verb, a subject pronoun, and
four possible conjugations. After you answer, an explanation for that
answer appears before you move on. At the end you see your score.

## What is covered

Four tenses: present indicative, preterite, imperfect, and future. Six
subject pronouns: eu, tu, ele/ela, nós, vocês, eles/elas. Roughly 50
hand-verified European Portuguese verbs, with an option to include or
exclude irregular verbs.

## Good to know

Lafa is free, with no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no ads.

There is no account and no login. The app opens straight into the quiz.

Lafa teaches European Portuguese as spoken in Portugal, not Brazilian
Portuguese.

## Also

Lafa works without a connection, using bundled quiz content when a network
fetch is not available. If you spot a problem with a question, you can
report it from inside the app.
