    Confirming calls the store's `reset()` and returns to Setup; declining leaves the

  <how-to-verify>
    1. Start the app on an iOS simulator (iPhone 15/16 class): `npx expo start` then press `i`.
    2. On Setup, select at least one tense and tap "Start Quiz" to reach an in-progress quiz.
    3. HEADER BUTTON: Confirm an "Exit" text control is visible in the top-left header. Tap it — a dialog titled to the effect of "Quit Quiz?" with "Keep Practicing" and "Quit Quiz" buttons must appear (NOT generic OK/Cancel).
    4. DECLINE: Tap "Keep Practicing". You must return to the EXACT same question you were on, with any selected answer / progress unchanged.
    5. SWIPE-BACK: Answer a question or two, then swipe from the left screen edge to go back. The SAME confirmation dialog must appear — the quiz must NOT silently disappear. Tap "Keep Practicing"; confirm you are back on the exact question.
    6. CONFIRM EXIT: Trigger the dialog again (button or swipe) and tap "Quit Quiz". You must land on the Setup screen with NO score / results / partial-results shown.
    7. CLEAN RESTART: From Setup, start a new quiz. It must begin at question 1 with a fresh progress bar and no leftover state from the abandoned session.
    8. NO DOUBLE-DIALOG: Play through to the final question and let the quiz auto-complete to the Results screen. Confirm no exit-confirmation dialog fires during that last-question → Results transition.
  </how-to-verify>
