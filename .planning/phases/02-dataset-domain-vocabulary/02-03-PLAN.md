  <what-built>

    4 tenses (present indicative, preterite, imperfect, future) x 6 subjects

    1. Open `src/dataset/verbs.ts`.
    2. Read each verb's four tense grids against your European-Portuguese knowledge
       (or a trusted reference). Pay closest attention to the irregulars
       (ser, estar, ter, ir, fazer, poder, querer, dizer, ver, dar, vir, saber, por,

    4. Confirm the mix reads as ~35-40 regular + ~10-15 irregular and that -ar/-er/-ir

       executor applies the corrections in `src/dataset/verbs.ts` and re-runs
