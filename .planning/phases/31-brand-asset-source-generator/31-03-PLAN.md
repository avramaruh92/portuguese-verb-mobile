<objective>
Prove the regenerated brand assets match the pre-refactor baseline (dimensions + alpha), get a
developer eyes-on confirmation that the shipped icon is genuinely the new Warm Bold Mark, and
reconcile the documented Phase 31 success criterion 4 with CONTEXT.md D-10's decision to defer the
real-EAS-build re-confirmation to Phase 35.

regression that only Apple's asset-catalog validation rejects, and a stale-icon false alarm where
the OS serves a cached icon and the developer wrongly concludes the regeneration failed. Both need
a human step. Separately, ROADMAP.md currently promises something in Phase 31 that D-10 explicitly
moved to Phase 35; leaving that unreconciled would make Phase 31 unverifiable as written.

Output: A verified phase and a roadmap/requirements record that matches what was actually decided.

  <what-built>
    `assets/brand/lafa-icon.svg` was promoted to the Warm Bold Mark with stable layer ids, the
    generator now extracts those layers by id, and all five PNG outputs were regenerated:
    `icon.png`, `favicon.png`, `splash-icon.png`, `android-icon-foreground.png`, and
    `android-icon-monochrome.png`.

    Visual changes to expect versus the previously shipped icon: a slightly larger, more saturated
    orange mark (0.96 wrapper scale on a bigger shape) plus a new soft offset depth shadow behind
    it. The depth shadow appears in the icon, splash, and Android foreground, and is deliberately
    absent from the Android monochrome silhouette.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    1. Open `assets/images/icon.png` and `assets/images/splash-icon.png` in Preview. Confirm both
       show the warm bold mark with a visible soft depth shadow offset down-and-right, and that the
       mark is fully inside the canvas with no clipping at the edges.
    2. Open `assets/images/android-icon-monochrome.png`. Confirm it is a clean solid-white
       silhouette on transparency with NO second ghosted/smudged offset copy behind it. A visible
       double-outline here means the depth layer leaked in and the phase is not done.
    3. Open `assets/images/android-icon-foreground.png`. Confirm the mark is centred and comfortably
       inside the safe zone (roughly the middle two-thirds of the square), not touching the edges.
    4. Open `assets/images/favicon.png`. Confirm the mark is still legible at 48x48 and not a
       blurry smudge.
    5. Delete the app from the simulator or device FIRST, then run `npm run ios` and reinstall.
       Do not skip the delete step: iOS aggressively caches app icons, and a stale cached icon is a
       known false-alarm source flagged in `.planning/research/PITFALLS.md` (pitfall 3). Confirm the
       home-screen icon is the new warm bold mark and the splash screen shows the new mark.
    6. Confirm the icon on the home screen has an opaque background (no dark/transparent corners
       bleeding through the OS rounding mask).
  </how-to-verify>
