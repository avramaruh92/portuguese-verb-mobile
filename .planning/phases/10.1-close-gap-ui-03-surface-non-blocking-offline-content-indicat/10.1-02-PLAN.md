<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 1: On-device verify of the "Using saved content" pill under fallback and non-fallback</name>
  <what-built>
    A session-wide "Using saved content" pill (neutral grey, secondary-token styled,
    non-interactive) that renders on the Setup, Quiz, and Results screens whenever
    the app falls back to the bundled local dataset because the remote fetch failed
    or was unreachable. Implemented in Plan 01 as `src/components/OfflinePill.tsx`,
    wired into all 3 screens.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    FALLBACK CASE (pill SHOULD appear):
    1. Enable Airplane Mode on the iOS device/simulator (this forces the remote
       verb fetch to fail, triggering the silent local fallback → source "local").
    2. Cold-launch the app (fully quit and reopen so `prefetch()` re-runs).
    3. On the Setup screen: confirm a small grey pill reading exactly
       "Using saved content" appears at the top of the body, above the
       "Portuguese Verb Quiz" heading. Confirm it is grey/neutral — NOT red,
       NOT an error banner — and that tapping it does nothing (not a button).
    4. Select at least one tense and tap "Start Quiz". On the Quiz screen:
       confirm the same pill appears below the header (Exit control still present
       and working), above the question/progress block.
    5. Complete or answer through to the Results screen. Confirm the same pill
       appears above the score number.

    NO-FALLBACK CASE (pill should NOT appear):
    6. Disable Airplane Mode (restore network) and cold-launch the app again.
    7. Confirm NO "Using saved content" pill appears on Setup, Quiz, or Results
       (remote fetch succeeded → source "remote").

    Report any wrong placement, wrong copy, error/red styling, tappable behavior,
    or a pill that appears/disappears mid-session incorrectly.
  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>Type "approved" if all 7 steps pass, or describe the visual/behavioral issues observed.</resume-signal>
</task>
