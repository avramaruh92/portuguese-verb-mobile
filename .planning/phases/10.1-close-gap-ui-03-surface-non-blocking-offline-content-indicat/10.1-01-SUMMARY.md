# Phase 10.1 Plan 01: OfflinePill Component + Screen Wiring Summary

One-liner: Session-wide "Using saved content" pill gated purely on `resolveVerbs()`'s memoized `source === "local"` result, rendered above the heading/question-block/score-block on Setup, Quiz, and Results respectively.

## What Was Built

- `src/components/OfflinePill.tsx`: a named-export `OfflinePill` component plus two pure exports — `OFFLINE_PILL_TEXT` (locked to the exact D-03 string "Using saved content") and `isLocalSource(source)` (true only for `"local"`). The component reads the dataset source via a `useState<VerbSource | null>` + `useEffect` that calls the existing memoized `resolveVerbs()` (never a second fetch, never `fetchRemoteVerbs()`/`resolve()` directly), guarded by an `active` flag to avoid setState-after-unmount. Renders `null` unless the source is `"local"`; otherwise a non-interactive `<View>/<Text>` pill styled only with `colors.secondary`, `colors.textSecondary`, `radius.control`, `spacing.sm`, `spacing.md` — zero new tokens, zero hardcoded hex, no `Pressable`/`onPress`.
- `__tests__/offline-pill.test.ts`: TDD-driven unit test (RED before GREEN) asserting `isLocalSource("local") === true`, `isLocalSource("remote") === false`, `isLocalSource(null) === false`, and `OFFLINE_PILL_TEXT === "Using saved content"` as a regression guard on the copy.
- Wired `<OfflinePill />` into all three screens at the UI-SPEC-fixed placements:
  - `app/index.tsx` (Setup): immediately before the "Portuguese Verb Quiz" heading, inside the main container.
  - `app/quiz.tsx` (Quiz): as the first child of the `ScrollView`, immediately before `progressRow`. The header's `headerLeft` Exit control was left untouched.
  - `app/results.tsx` (Results): immediately after the main-return `Stack.Screen`, before `scoreBlock` — not added to the no-session fallback branch, per UI-SPEC scoping.
