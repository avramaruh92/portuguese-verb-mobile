<objective>
Implement FETCH-05: a small, non-blocking, session-wide "Using saved content"
pill that appears on the Setup, Quiz, and Results screens whenever the dataset
`source` resolved by Phase 7's `resolveVerbs()` is `"local"` (i.e. the remote
fetch failed/was unreachable/returned invalid data and the app silently fell
back to the bundled dataset).

Purpose: Close the v0.1 milestone-audit gap on UI-03 — the fully-computed-but-
never-read `source: "local"` signal from `src/dataset/source.ts` finally becomes
visible to the learner as a passive, informational indicator, WITHOUT re-opening
FETCH-03's silent-fallback behavior (no new error state, no thrown error, no
blocking).

Output: One new shared component (`src/components/OfflinePill.tsx`) with a pure,
unit-tested source gate + locked copy constant, plus three one-line screen wirings.
</objective>
