    A new "Help us improve" product-feedback flow: a footer link on Setup and Results, a two-action row on Quiz ("Report a problem" + "Help us improve"), and a shared ProductFeedbackModal (category pills Bug/Idea/Other, required message, Submit) that POSTs to /product-feedback with no quiz-answer context.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    Launch the app on the iOS simulator or a device (`npm run ios`), then:

    1. SETUP screen: Confirm a small, low-visual-weight "Help us improve" link appears BELOW the "Start Quiz" button (caption size, accent-colored text, no button fill). Tap it — the "Send feedback" modal opens. Confirm the Submit button is disabled until you type a message. Pick a category, type a message, Submit — confirm "✓ Feedback sent — thank you!" then auto-close (~1.5s).

    2. QUIZ screen: Start a quiz. BEFORE locking an answer, confirm "Help us improve" is already visible/tappable and "Report a problem" is NOT visible. Lock an answer — confirm "Report a problem" now appears beside "Help us improve" as an equal-weight, half-width button, and the row does NOT jump/reflow when it appears. Tap "Help us improve" — the modal opens with no quiz-answer text anywhere in it. Tap "Report a problem" — confirm the existing (unchanged) report flow still works.

    3. RESULTS screen: Complete a quiz. Confirm "Help us improve" appears BELOW the Share Score / Try Again / Back to Setup group as the same footer link. Tap it and submit feedback successfully.

    4. NON-BLOCKING: At no point should opening/closing the feedback modal block or reset quiz progress.

    5. (Optional error-path spot check) If convenient, submit with airplane mode on to confirm the modal shows "Something went wrong. Please try again." with a "Retry submission" button, and quiz progress is unaffected.
  </how-to-verify>
