<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 1: Visually confirm the app icon and splash mark in a running build</name>
  <what-built>
    Plan 01 generated `assets/images/icon.png` (1024x1024, alpha-free, Lafa 'a' mark bled
    edge-to-edge on peach #FCE4DA) and `assets/images/splash-icon.png` (white monochrome 'a'
    silhouette, no green dot, transparent background). Plan 02 removed the Icon Composer bundle
    so the flat PNG is the sole iOS icon source; the splash plugin still composites the glyph over
    the unchanged #208AEF blue background.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    1. Start a build that shows the native icon and splash. Either:
       - `npx expo run:ios` (a local prebuild/dev build shows the real home-screen icon + splash), or
       - if a full native build is not convenient, open `assets/images/icon.png` and
         `assets/images/splash-icon.png` directly (Preview/Finder) AND launch the app in the iOS
         simulator to observe the splash screen at startup.
    2. App icon — confirm ALL of:
       - The orange Lafa 'a' mark fills the icon (bled to the edges), on the light peach background.
       - No "lafa" wordmark text is present, and there is NO inset white margin / icon-within-an-icon look.
       - The mark is legible at actual home-screen size.
    3. Splash screen — confirm ALL of:
       - A clean WHITE 'a' silhouette appears centered on the blue (#208AEF) background at launch.
       - No green accent dot is present (D-11), and the mark is not blurry/soft on the device.
    4. If anything is wrong (wrong crop, alpha halo, blurry splash, green dot visible, colors off),
       describe the defect — it will be fixed by re-running/adjusting the plan 01 script (script
       constants or the splash multiplier), not by hand-editing the PNGs.
  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>Type "approved" if both icon and splash look correct, or describe the specific visual issues to fix.</resume-signal>
</task>
