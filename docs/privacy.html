Lafa Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-17

Summary

Lafa has no accounts, collects no personal information, and uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Lafa does not track users across other apps or websites.

What Lafa sends to its server

Lafa's server-side network calls are limited to the following three:

  • POST /feedback — sent only when you submit a "Report a problem" for a quiz question. Fields: message, verb, tense, subject, correctAnswer, selectedAnswer, appVersion, platform.
  • POST /product-feedback — sent only when you submit general in-app feedback. Fields: category, message, screen, appVersion, platform.
  • GET /content/verbs — fetches quiz content on startup. No body, no authentication, and no identifiers are sent.

The two feedback calls only ever happen when you actively choose to submit a report or feedback message.

What is stored

For the two feedback calls, only the submitted fields listed above are stored, along with a generated id and a timestamp. No IP address, user agent, or device identifier is stored. Your quiz answers and scores stay on your device in memory only and are never uploaded.

What Lafa does NOT collect

Lafa does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, health data, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, purchase history, or browsing history.

Free-text feedback

The feedback message field is text you type yourself. Please avoid including personal information in it.

Third parties

Lafa's backend runs on Render and stores data in a Supabase-hosted Postgres database, used only as infrastructure providers. Lafa does not sell data, share data for advertising, or use data for profiling.

Children

Lafa is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
